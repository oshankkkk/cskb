#### Pitches and tempo
Pitch is how high or low a sound or musical note is and is determined by the frequency of its sound wave: higher frequencies produce higher pitches, while lower frequencies produce lower pitches. Tempo, on the other hand, is the speed of the music or beat and is measured in beats per minute (BPM). A piece of music can have a high or low pitch regardless of whether its tempo is fast or slow, since pitch and tempo are independent of each other.
