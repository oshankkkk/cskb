### Intro
So calc was formed to deal with infinity. The Zenos paradox tells that you cant walk to the other side of the room cause the distance can be divided into halfs infinite times and since infinity cannot exits, like you first have to walk half the distance, then half of what remains, then half again. Therefore one cannot go from 1 side of the room to another. But since it is physically possible it means that we can get finite results out of infinite opertations. 

People in Greece found areas of shapes by making smaller polygons within the shape using its vertices, since the circle has no verticies to find the circle they made a triangle inside it and increased its vertieces, and got the equation that when the vertices are infinte the area of the circle is similar to the area of the polygon inside it.

Issac Newton and some other smart people made calcules which are a set of mathematical systems/tools to work with problems that deals with infinity.
- limits
- instantaneous rates of change
- derivatives
- areas under curves
- infinite series
- continuous motion

infinity → limits → derivatives → differential equations → modeling continuous systems.
### Differentiation
Differentiation is finding the gradient of a curve at a certian point with a tanget line and getting its points and slowly moving it to the point that we want while taking its gradient along each point and finally ending up with the gradient of the point we want.
